Lamborghini Miami

While familiar Lamborghini models such as the Aventador and Huracan are certainly at the forefront of many Florida drivers minds, sometimes it’s the behind-the-scenes models that get might go unnoticed. After all, long before the Aventador and Huracan were household names, they were mere concepts. Years on, they carry the banner for the Lamborghini Automobili nameplate, and show no signs of stopping. So, what’s next on the horizon for Lamborghini? Two models shown here, the Asterion and Estoque, might just be the next future legends to emerge from the Lamborghini drawing board. Each exemplifies the most basic tenets of the Lamborghini brand -- superb performance, esoteric design, trendsetting innovation -- yet takes drivers to a destination that no current production model can. We look forward to seeing models such as the Asterion and Estoque in the Prestige Imports showroom in the years to come, and anticipate even further advancements from as-yet-unnamed Lamborghini concept models on the road ahead.

Getting to Know the Lamborghini Asterion and Estoque

So what will these engaging concepts have in store for South Florida drivers if and when they arrive on our roads? Starting with the Asterion concept, this represents a new horizon for Lamborghini performance: Plug-in Hybrid technology. In addition to offering the PHEV option, drivers will also have the option of employing all-electric power during urban driving situations. From a design standpoint, meanwhile, the Asterion eschews some of the more rigid surfaces of its siblings, and instead exhibits curvaceous, free-flowing lines, with forged carbon fiber, aluminum and titanium highlights. For its part, the Estoque pushes the envelope even further away from Lamborghini’s current offerings. The Estoque is the first-ever four-door super sports car from Lamborghini, designed to present buyers with high performance and drivability in a single car. Its long wheelbase, wide track and oversize wheels represent a bold step forward for Lamborghini, and will certainly appeal to an entirely new subset of supercar enthusiast.

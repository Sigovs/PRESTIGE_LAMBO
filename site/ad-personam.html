The process

From Miami to Sant’Agata

It begins with a conversation with our sales team in Miami: the model, the colours, the materials and the details you have in mind.

The specification is then developed with Lamborghini’s Ad Personam team, and a visit to the Ad Personam Studio in Sant’Agata Bolognese can be arranged through us.

Request a consultation

Step by step

Four steps to your car

  • Talk to us

    A conversation with our sales team in Miami about the car you have in mind.

  • Configure

    Colours and options in the Lamborghini configurator, with us in the showroom or at home.

  • Specify

    The specification is developed with Lamborghini’s Ad Personam team; a Studio visit in Sant’Agata can be arranged through us.

  • Delivery

    Your car is built to that specification and delivered through Lamborghini Miami.

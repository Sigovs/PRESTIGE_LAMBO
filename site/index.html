Lamborghini Miami

Since 1977

Lamborghini Miami is part of Prestige Imports, the family dealership Irv David founded in Miami in 1977. It began at a gas station he owned, grew into a dealership for European cars, and in 1986 opened its own showroom on Biscayne Boulevard.

Since 2007 the house has been led by his son, Brett David. In 2011 Brett and his sister, Brooke, created the Ride2Revive Foundation.

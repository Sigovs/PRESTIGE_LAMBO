About this vehicle

2024 Lamborghini Revuelto – The Future of Supercar Performance

Prestige Imports is thrilled to present this extraordinary 2024 Lamborghini Revuelto, representing the pinnacle of Italian automotive engineering and the dawn of a new era for the legendary marque. With just 1,414 carefully driven miles, this virtually new hypercar delivers an intoxicating blend of naturally aspirated V12 fury and cutting-edge hybrid technology.

Groundbreaking Performance

At the heart of this masterpiece lies a magnificent 6.5-liter V12 DOHC 48V engine, complemented by dual front and single rear electric motors, creating a revolutionary plug-in hybrid powertrain that redefines what's possible in a supercar. The sophisticated 8-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission with driver-selectable modes and sequential shift control delivers lightning-fast gear changes, while the full-time all-wheel-drive system ensures maximum traction and control. The advanced regenerative braking system with ceramic discs provides fade-free stopping power worthy of this machine's capabilities.

Cutting-Edge Technology

The Revuelto features an 8.4-inch vertical touchscreen with Amazon Alexa integration, Apple CarPlay, and Android Auto compatibility, seamlessly blending supercar performance with modern connectivity. The over-the-air navigation updates ensure you're always equipped with the latest mapping technology. Dual LCD monitors keep vital information at your fingertips, while the wireless phone connectivity and mobile hotspot keep you connected on every journey.

Exquisite Craftsmanship

Step inside to discover premium leather seat trim with meticulous contrast stitching, carbon fiber instrument panel inserts, and Corsa-Tex leather/simulated suede accents throughout. The comfort-focused seats provide exceptional support during spirited driving, while the dual-zone automatic climate control ensures perfect cabin conditions.

Advanced Engineering

Magnetic fluid-filled shock absorbers and adaptive suspension with driver-selectable ride control deliver exceptional handling dynamics. The 20-inch front and 21-inch rear Venancio shiny silver wheels wrapped in performance run-flat tires complete the aggressive stance.

Our history in pictures About us

Over 45 years ago, Prestige Imports established itself as a leader and innovator of the luxury automobile world.

Founded by Irv David, upon principles of excellent service and personal commitment to customer satisfaction, it has since expanded into one of the most successful auto groups in the country. Before Prestige Imports became what it is today, the business started from a gas station that Mr. David owned evolving into a dealership selling used European cars. Mr. David envisioned selling more than the typical vehicles and invested his time and energy in what he saw as an untapped market, but his drive and enthusiasm paid off. He made his dream a reality by becoming a pioneer in the exotic car market opening one of the first exotic car dealerships in Florida.

Upon Irv David’s tragic passing in 2007, his then 19‑year‑old son, Brett, was faced with a choice between remaining an heir to a budding empire or assuming his father’s position as CEO of Prestige Imports.

Brett David along with his team at Prestige Imports has now led the company to unparalleled growth and success while gaining new levels of achievement. Prestige Imports proudly counts itself among the most successful luxury automobile companies in the country. Currently, Prestige Imports is thriving under its corporate wings with premier franchises: Lamborghini Miami, which famously introduced the Sharpie Lamborghini and AU79 Aventador; Pagani of Miami, one of only three licensed dealers in the country, Lotus of Miami, offering the Evora, Exige, and Elise; and most recently KARMA Miami with the new Revero due for deliveries.

Prestige Imports has expanded the vision set forth by the late Irv David.

The company is internationally renowned for its success with an array of various ventures and nontraditional projects. Prestige Imports is always thinking of the next cutting-edge idea, to go beyond expectations that will set off the industry. Prestige Imports recently introduced Exotics & Espresso, a monthly meet & greet event that brings together exotic car owners, enthusiasts, and other members of the South Florida community. The family-owned business also participates in countless fundraising events for local charities such as MyWish4U, Joe DiMaggio Children’s Hospital, and Chai Lifeline. In memory of his father, Brett teamed with the American Heart Association to develop the Prestigious Heart Fund, a non-profit dedicated to promoting heart health and awareness. In 2011, Brett, along with his sister Brooke, created the Ride2Revive Foundation which provides children who are undergoing medical treatment for life-threatening diseases, with memorable adrenaline-filled excursions, meant to distract them from their health-related struggles and revitalize their senses.

Located on Biscayne Boulevard in the heart of North Miami Beach, Prestige Imports has a collective inventory that intrinsically fits the South Florida lifestyle. Whether you are in the market to buy, lease or trade, Prestige Imports’ team of world-class agents will help you obtain the proper vehicle for your exact needs. In addition to Audi, Lamborghini, Pagani, and Lotus, you will find a revolving selection of Certified Pre-Owned vehicles featuring brands such as Aston Martin, Bentley, BMW, Ferrari, Jaguar, Land Rover, Maserati, Mercedes Benz, Porsche, and Rolls Royce.

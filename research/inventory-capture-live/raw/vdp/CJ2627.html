ABOUT THIS VEHICLE

**2025 Lamborghini Revuelto – The Future of Italian Supercar Excellence**

Prestige Imports is thrilled to present this extraordinary 2025 Lamborghini Revuelto, representing the pinnacle of hybrid hypercar engineering. With only 2,085 miles on the odometer, this virtually new masterpiece (Stock #ATA01922, VIN: ZHWUC1ZM5SLA01922) comes with both CARFAX Clean One Owner and AUTOCHECK Clean reports, ensuring complete peace of mind.

**Groundbreaking Hybrid Performance**

At the heart of this beast lies a revolutionary 6.5-liter V12 DOHC 48V engine paired with dual front and single rear electric motors, delivering an intoxicating blend of raw power and cutting-edge efficiency through an 8-speed dual-clutch automatic transmission. The full-time all-wheel-drive system with electro-mechanical limited slip differential ensures every ounce of power reaches the pavement with precision. The 3.8 kWh lithium-ion traction battery enables regenerative braking and electric-only driving capabilities, making this the most advanced Lamborghini ever created.

**Track-Ready Engineering**

Experience uncompromising control with magnetic fluid-filled shock absorbers, adaptive sport-tuned suspension with driver-selectable ride height, and ceramic disc brakes with 4-wheel ABS. The double wishbone suspension front and rear, combined with front and rear anti-roll bars, delivers razor-sharp handling worthy of the Raging Bull badge.

**Stunning Design & Technology**

Rolling on exclusive 20-inch front and 21-inch rear Venancio Shiny Silver wheels wrapped in performance run-flat summer tires, this Revuelto commands attention from every angle. The power spoiler, composite/aluminum panels, and aggressive aerodynamics showcase pure Italian artistry.

Inside, luxurious leather seat trim envelops you in comfort, while the 8.4-inch infotainment touchscreen features **Apple CarPlay** and **Android Auto** integration, navigation, and wireless connectivity. The driver-focused cockpit includes carbon fiber instrument panel inserts, simulated suede accents, and a sport leather steering wheel with sequential shift controls.

**Safety & Convenience**

Advanced safety features include active lane departure warning, backup camera, dual-stage airbags throughout, electronic stability control, and emergency SOS capability. Proximity key entry with push-button start, dual-zone automatic climate control, and power-folding heated mirrors add everyday usability to this exotic machine.

This is your opportunity to own automotive history. Contact Prestige Imports today to experience the 2025 Lamborghini Revuelto.
